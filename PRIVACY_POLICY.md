2. Information We Collect

When you use Kagami, the bot may process information associated with your Discord account and your use of Kagami.

2.1 Discord User ID

Kagami stores your Discord user ID.

This identifier is used to associate your Kagami account with your economy data.

Kagami does not need your Discord password or Discord authentication token to provide its normal economy features.

---

2.2 Economy Data

Kagami may store information such as:

- Wallet balance
- Bank balance
- Inventory items
- Daily reward streak
- Daily reward cooldown
- Work cooldown
- Crime cooldown
- Rob cooldown
- Beg cooldown
- Fishing cooldown
- Coin-flip cooldown
- Other economy-related information required by future features

This information is necessary to operate Kagami's global economy and prevent abuse of economy mechanics.

---

2.3 Account Timestamps

Kagami may store technical timestamps associated with your Kagami account, including account creation and update timestamps.

These timestamps help maintain and operate the database.

---

2.4 Discord Server Information

Kagami may have access to information made available through Discord that is necessary for operating commands in a server.

Depending on the command and Discord's permissions, this may include:

- Discord server identifiers
- Server membership information
- User identifiers
- Information needed to respond to commands

Kagami does not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for its functionality.

---

2.5 Command Usage

Kagami processes commands you send to the bot so that it can perform the requested action.

For example, when you use:

,balance

Kagami needs to identify your Discord account and retrieve your economy data to display your balance.

Kagami may process command-related information temporarily while handling a request.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Kagami does not intentionally request or store the following as part of its normal economy functionality:

- Discord passwords
- Discord authentication tokens
- MongoDB passwords

- Bank account information

- Precise physical location
