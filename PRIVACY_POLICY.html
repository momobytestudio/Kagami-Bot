🔒 Kagami — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026
Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kagami collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when you use the Kagami Discord bot.

Kagami is a Discord bot providing entertainment and global economy features.

Kagami aims to process only information reasonably necessary to operate and maintain the service.

1. Who We Are

Kagami is operated by its development team.

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, contact the Kagami development team through the official Kagami privacy contact.

Privacy Contact: [ADD KAGAMI PRIVACY CONTACT]

2. Information We Collect

When you use Kagami, the bot may process information associated with your Discord account and your use of Kagami.

2.1 Discord User ID

Kagami stores your Discord user ID.

This identifier is used to associate your Kagami account with your economy data.

2.2 Economy Data

Kagami may store:

  • Wallet balance
  • Bank balance
  • Inventory items
  • Daily reward streak
  • Daily reward cooldown
  • Work cooldown
  • Crime cooldown
  • Rob cooldown
  • Beg cooldown
  • Fishing cooldown
  • Coin-flip cooldown
  • Other economy-related information required by future features

2.3 Account Timestamps

Kagami may store technical timestamps associated with your Kagami account, including account creation and update timestamps.

2.4 Discord Server Information

Kagami may process information made available through Discord that is necessary for operating commands in a server.

Depending on the command and Discord permissions, this may include:

  • Discord server identifiers
  • Server membership information
  • User identifiers
  • Information needed to respond to commands

2.5 Command Usage

Kagami processes commands you send to the bot so that it can perform the requested action.

Kagami may temporarily process command-related information while handling a request.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Kagami does not intentionally request or store the following as part of its normal economy functionality:

  • Discord passwords
  • Discord authentication tokens
  • MongoDB passwords
  • Payment card information
  • Bank account information
  • Government identification numbers
  • Real-world financial account information
  • Precise physical location
  • Contact lists

You should never provide passwords, authentication tokens, private keys, or other authentication credentials to Kagami.

4. How We Use Information

Kagami uses information for purposes including:

  • Operating Kagami
  • Identifying your Kagami account
  • Maintaining wallet and bank balances
  • Maintaining inventory
  • Tracking cooldowns
  • Tracking reward streaks
  • Processing economy commands
  • Displaying profiles
  • Generating leaderboards
  • Preventing duplicate rewards
  • Maintaining economy integrity
  • Detecting abuse
  • Investigating exploits
  • Correcting incorrectly generated currency
  • Protecting the bot and its users
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Maintaining database integrity

Kagami does not intentionally use your economy information for unrelated advertising purposes.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, Kagami processes personal information in accordance with the lawful bases available under applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

Depending on the processing activity, processing may be necessary to provide and operate the requested service, comply with legal obligations, protect legitimate interests, or rely on another lawful basis recognized by applicable law.

6. Database and Storage

Kagami currently uses MongoDB to store economy information.

Information may therefore be stored using third-party cloud database or infrastructure services.

Kagami's development team will take reasonable steps to limit access to stored information to what is necessary to operate the service.

7. Data Sharing

Kagami does not sell your personal information.

Kagami does not intentionally share your personal information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary for:

  • Operating Kagami
  • Hosting or database services
  • Security
  • Abuse prevention
  • Technical support
  • Legal compliance
  • Responding to lawful requests
  • Protecting Kagami, its users, or third parties

8. Public Information

Some Kagami features may display information publicly within Discord.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Discord display names
  • Discord avatars
  • Wallet balances
  • Bank balances
  • Total wealth
  • Inventory counts
  • Leaderboard positions
  • Other information intentionally displayed by an economy command

A global leaderboard may allow other Kagami users to see economy-related information associated with participating users.

9. Data Minimization

Kagami is designed to collect and process information relevant to its functionality.

If a future feature requires additional categories of personal information, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain that processing.

10. Data Security

Kagami takes reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect stored information from unauthorized access, disclosure, accidental loss, unauthorized modification, destruction, and other inappropriate processing.

Security may include appropriate access controls, credential protection, database security practices, and restricted access to development infrastructure.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

Kagami retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, security, operational, and dispute-resolution requirements.

Economy information may need to be retained while your Kagami account remains active so that Kagami can provide its global economy features.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, Kagami may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law.
